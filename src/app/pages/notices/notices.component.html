Your Data and Permissions

Google Calendar add-on: TOAB Jitsi Addon

When you install TOAB Jitsi Plugin for Google Calendar, Google asks you to grant the permissions below. This is what each one is for.

  • We only use your calendar data to add and manage Jitsi meetings. We never store the titles, descriptions, times or guests of your events, and we never access your contacts or email.
  • We never sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • You can uninstall the plugin and remove its access at any time, and ask us to delete your data.

TOAB Jitsi Plugin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. For full details, read our Privacy Policy.

Cal.com app: toab_jitsi

When you install TOAB Jitsi for Cal.com and add it to an event type, the app gets access to the data below. This is what each item is for.

  • We only use your Cal.com data to add and manage Jitsi meetings. We never send our servers your bookings' titles, descriptions, times, attendee names or emails, or booking answers, and we never access your calendars or contacts.
  • We never sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • You can uninstall the app and remove its access at any time, and ask us to delete your data.

For full details, read our Privacy Policy.
