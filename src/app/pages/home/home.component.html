TOAB Jitsi Plugin

Jitsi Meet video conferencing for your calendar

TOAB Jitsi Plugin adds a Jitsi Meet video conference to the calendar events you create. Choose one of our curated public Jitsi servers for each meeting. Support for your own self-hosted Jitsi server and custom room names is coming soon.
